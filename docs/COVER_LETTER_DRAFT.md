# Cover letter draft — JHEP

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript “A Computer-Assisted All-Orders Certificate
for a Six-Field Flux Subsystem on a Calabi–Yau Threefold” for publication in
the *Journal of High Energy Physics*.

The paper gives a computer-assisted proof of existence and local uniqueness
of a supersymmetric root of the axiodilaton plus five-complex-structure
closed-flux subsystem for the explicit toric Calabi–Yau dataset `5-81-3213`.
The central advance is not another high-precision numerical solution. The
proof accounts for all 557 finite source rows using exact Chow arithmetic,
partitions all 8150 low-total representations explicitly, bounds the remaining
infinite GKZ tail, and propagates it through the periods, F-terms, real
Jacobian, and a 12-dimensional interval Krawczyk operator. A small independent
verifier recomputes the final rational inequalities and rejects altered
witnesses.

To our knowledge, this is the first end-to-end interval certificate of local
existence and uniqueness for an explicit multi-parameter compact
Calabi–Yau closed-flux root that includes both a complete finite GKZ sector
and a rigorously propagated all-orders infinite tail. The manuscript carefully
does not claim stabilization of the 81 Kähler moduli, open-string fields, a
full 113-field root, or a complete physical vacuum.

The code, exact data, certificate, and reproducibility instructions are
deposited in the immutable Zenodo archive
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21906027. This work is
original, is not under consideration elsewhere, and has been approved by all
authors.

Suggested expertise for review includes interval arithmetic/computer-assisted
proofs and computational mirror symmetry/type-IIB flux compactifications.
